Pizza is a delicious and popular Italian dish made with fresh dough, tomato sauce, and melted cheese.
It has a crispy crust and a soft, cheesy center.

It is easy to make at home and perfect for sharing with family and friends.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 teaspoon dry yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sakt
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella chease
  • fresh basil leaves
  • 1 teaspoon oregano

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, mix the flour, dry yeast, and salt.
  2. Add the warm water and olive oil, then mix until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough for about 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rise for about 1 hour.
  5. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  6. Roll out the dough into a round pizza shape.
  7. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  8. Sprinkle the shredded mozzarella cheese on top.
  9. Add oregano and fresh basil leaves.
  10. Bake the pizza for 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  11. Remove the pizza from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes.
  12. Slice the pizza and serve hot.
