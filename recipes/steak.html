Improve Your Dinner

Steak is a delicious and juicy meat dish that is full of flavor. It is seasoned with salt and pepper and cooked until perfectly tender.

Steak is a great choice for a special dinner and can be served with potatoes or vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 ribeye steak
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 spring fresh rosemary
  • 1 teaspoon paprika

Steps

  1. Remove the steak from the refrigerator about 30 minutes before cooking.
  2. Pat the steak dry with paper towels.
  3. Season both sides of the steak with salt, black pepper, and paprika.
  4. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. Place the steak in the hot skillet and cook for 3–5 minutes on each side, depending on its thickness.
  6. Add the garlic and fresh rosemary to the skillet.
  7. Spoon the hot oil over the steak to add flavor.
  8. Check the internal temperature with a meat thermometer to reach your preferred doneness.
  9. Remove the steak from the skillet and let it rest for 5–10 minutes.
  10. Slice the steak against the grain.
  11. Serve the steak hot with your favorite side dishes.
