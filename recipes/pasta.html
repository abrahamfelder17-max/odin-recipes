The Perfect Lunch

Pasta is a traditional Italian dish that is simple and delicious.

This recipe combines tender pasta with a rich tomato sauce and flavorful seasonings.
It is easy to prepare and makes a perfect meal for lunch or dinner.

ingredients

  • 2 cups penne pasta
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • 1/2 cup chopped tomatoes

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil.
  2. Add salt to the boiling water.
  3. Add the penne pasta and cook for 10–12 minutes until tender.
  4. Drain the pasta and set it aside.
  5. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  6. Add the chopped garlic and cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
  7. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook for 3–4 minutes.
  8. Pour the tomato sauce into the skillet and stir well.
  9. Add salt, black pepper, and oregano to the sauce.
  10. Let the sauce simmer for 5–7 minutes.
  11. Add the cooked pasta to the skillet and mix until evenly coated with sauce.
  12. Sprinkle grated Parmesan cheese over the pasta.
  13. Garnish with fresh basil leaves.
  14. Serve the pasta hot and enjoy!
